One-line: a 2×2 of short problem statements, for naming the pain before introducing the product.

```jsx
<ProblemGrid items={[
  { title: "Vendor lock-in", body: "Engine APIs in business classes tie your release schedule to someone else's." },
  { title: "Big-bang risk", body: "A cut-over means a freeze window and a rollback plan nobody wants to test." },
]} />
```

Two lines per item, maximum. If an item needs a paragraph it belongs in prose, not here.
