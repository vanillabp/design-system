One-line: the VanillaBP logo — inline mark, or the compact / full outline lockups whose wordmark follows `currentColor`.

```jsx
<Logo variant="full" size={138} src="assets/logo/vanillabp-logo-currentcolor.svg" />
<Logo variant="mark" size={48} />
```

Never recolour, rotate, outline or stretch the mark, and never set the wordmark in another face. Minimum sizes are hard: mark 48px, compact 130px, full 234px wide. Headers use `full` at rest and swap to `compact` once scrolled — do not shrink the full lockup.
