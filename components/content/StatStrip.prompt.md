One-line: the figures under a hero that make the claim concrete.

```jsx
<StatStrip stats={[
  { value: "2", label: "engines supported today, on the same business code" },
  { value: "0", label: "lines of business logic to rewrite when you switch" },
]} />
```

Every figure must be verifiable from the repositories. A stat you cannot point at is data slop — drop it rather than round it.
