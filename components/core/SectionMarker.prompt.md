One-line: BPMN start/task/end glyph standing in for a section number — use it only when the sections really are a sequence the reader moves through.

```jsx
<SectionMarker type="start" />
<Eyebrow marker="task">Blueprints</Eyebrow>
```

2px stroke at 20px, drawn in `--accent-graphic`. Not for unordered content, and never alongside 01/02/03 numbering.
